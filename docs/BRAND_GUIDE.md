# SolFoundry Brand Guide

Welcome to the SolFoundry Brand Guide. This document outlines the official typography, color palette, and logo usage guidelines to ensure a consistent brand identity across all integrations and community projects.

## 1. Core Identity
**SolFoundry** is the decentralized forge for builders. Our brand communicates reliability, innovation, and open collaboration.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary Forge Orange:** `#FF5A00` (Used for primary CTAs, links, and highlights)
- **Deep Steel Blue:** `#0F172A` (Primary background for dark mode, text for light mode)
- **Anvil Gray:** `#64748B` (Secondary text, borders, and subtle UI elements)
- **Success Green:** `#10B981` (Used for verified bounties and successful payouts)

## 3. Typography
- **Headings:** `Space Grotesk` (Sans-serif, geometric, futuristic)
- **Body Text:** `Inter` (Clean, highly legible sans-serif for UI density)
- **Monospace:** `JetBrains Mono` (For code snippets, wallet addresses, and token amounts)

## 4. Logo Usage
- **Clear Space:** Always maintain a clear space around the logo equal to half the logo's height.
- **Minimum Size:** Do not render the logo smaller than `24px` in height.
- **Monochrome:** When using the logo on complex backgrounds, use the pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pure black (`#000000`) variants.

## 5. Tone of Voice
- **Technical but Accessible:** We speak to developers and creators. Use precise technical terms when necessary, but keep the overarching message clear and welcoming.
- **Action-Oriented:** Use active verbs (e.g., "Forge your path", "Claim bounty", "Deploy contract").
